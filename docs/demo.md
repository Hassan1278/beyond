# Demo

**Site.** `/#/` is the marketing site.

- "Try one night" and the Patterns section use fictional, scripted content.
- They never call the backend and forget what a visitor types.

**Two versions of the journal.** The first page of `/app` offers both side by side, and the top bar has a **Demo / Live** switch on every page.

- **Demo** needs no account and no AI. It opens on Kit's fictional journal (a few months of letters, labelled "sample", with pre-written month and year notes) and walks through one night: write, seal, **Skip to morning**, open the letter, look around. A link straight into it: `/app?demo`.
- **Live** is a Beyond account. Letters are written by the AI service from what you actually wrote.

In the demo:

- Everything lives only in the page. Reloading starts the demo over, and nothing reaches the backend or the AI service.
- **Skip to morning** plays the overnight animation and opens a sealed letter right away.
- A night you seal gets a pre-written letter a few seconds later, labelled "demo letter", chosen from what was written (feeling alone, not knowing what's next, study pressure, family, tiredness, a good day or an ordinary day) and quoting the writer's own sentences. It notices interests such as helping others or building things, which also feed Identity. A night that sounds like a crisis gets a support letter with Hong Kong hotlines instead. English, 繁體 and 简体 are covered.
- Month and year notes in Patterns are also pre-written, built from the letters' own quotes.
- **Fill in an example night** on Tonight types a fictional night, for a quick run-through.

**Recording a demo.**

- **Without AI keys:** use the demo. Kit's sample covers the morning, Patterns and Identity views, and any night you seal gets a demo letter.
- **With keys:** switch to Live, sign in, seal a night, wait about a minute for the scheduled writer (or use **Read it now**), and the real letter arrives.

Use a fictional account and fictional words when recording. In Live, everything you seal is saved to that account.
