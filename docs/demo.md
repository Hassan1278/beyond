# Demo

**Site.** `/#/` is the marketing site.

- "Try one night" and the Patterns section use fictional, scripted content.
- They never call the backend and forget what a visitor types.

**Journal: Demo mode and Normal mode.** Both need a Beyond account, and in both, letters are written by the AI service.

- **Demo mode** is the default. The sign-in page says so, and Settings → Mode switches to Normal mode. The setting is remembered on that device.
  - Tonight offers six fictional **sample nights**, one per theme: feeling alone, not knowing what's next, study pressure (简体), family expectations (粵語), helping others, an ordinary good day. Tapping one fills the page.
  - Sealing it saves it to the account like any other night, and the AI writes its letter, usually within a minute. **Skip to morning** opens it right away.
  - Kit's fictional letters (a few months of them, labelled "sample", with pre-written month and year notes) appear next to your own, so Letters, Identity and Patterns have something to show.
- **Normal mode** is just your own journal: no sample nights and no Kit.

**Recording a demo.** Sign in with a fictional account, stay in Demo mode, tap a sample night, seal it and skip to morning. Without AI keys the night waits as "queued" and no letter appears; Kit's sample letters still cover Letters, Patterns and Identity. Everything you seal is saved to that account.
